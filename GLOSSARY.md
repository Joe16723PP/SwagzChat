# SwagzChat

The words for a chat between people in SwagzChat.

## Language

**Guest**:
A person who opens SwagzChat with a name and no account. The same browser keeps that guest.
_Avoid_: Account, signed-in user

**Conversation**:
A single chat history with a fixed kind and a list of members.
_Avoid_: Channel, room, thread, chat log

**Kind**:
How a conversation was created: own, friend, or group. It stays as it was created.
_Avoid_: Channel type, chat type

**Own conversation**:
A conversation whose only member is that user.
_Avoid_: Owner chat, saved messages, notes channel

**Friend conversation**:
A conversation created between two friends.
_Avoid_: Direct message, DM, private chat

**Group conversation**:
A conversation created with three or more members, and a name.
_Avoid_: Room, channel

**Member**:
A user who can read and write in a conversation.
_Avoid_: Participant, subscriber

**Owner**:
The member who may add members, remove members, and rename a group. Every conversation has one.
_Avoid_: Admin, creator, own

**Friendship**:
A mutual link between two users. It is separate from membership in a conversation.
_Avoid_: Contact, follower
